ChisBurner

GBA ROM high-address read-only field diagnostic.

This build permits exactly two passes over eleven fixed 32-byte ROM windows:
the ChisBurner power-of-two mirror-probe sequence from 0x000000A0 through
0x010000A0, plus 0x01FFFFE0. It reports the observed mirror size as information
and requires every sampled byte to repeat. Cartridge writes and save-memory
transactions are unavailable.
